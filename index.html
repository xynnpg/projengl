What does success really mean?

Success beyond money & fame.

A six-minute reflection on achievement, failure, perseverance, and the life we choose to build.

English Project · 11th Grade 6–8 minutes · 8 chapters

01 Define it

00:45

Look past the scoreboard.

Success is bigger
than one metric.

Money can buy options. Fame can open doors. Neither tells us whether a person is growing, connected, or fulfilled.

03 Reframe it

00:55

The first result is rarely the final result.

Failure ≠ finish line.

Try→Rejection→Lesson→Better try→Breakthrough
01

Basketball

Lesson: let disappointment fuel practice.

Writing

Failure and rejection changed her path before Harry Potter found its readers.

Lesson: a closed door is not the whole story.

progress toward goals
